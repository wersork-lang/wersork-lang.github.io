hp-backup privacy policy

hp-backup is used only by its owner, for the owner's own Google account.

  • It uses the Google Drive scope drive.file: it can see only the files it created itself.
  • It uploads the owner's backups, encrypted on the owner's server before upload, to the owner's own Google Drive.
  • No data is collected, shared, sold or sent to anyone else. No analytics, no advertising.
  • Access can be removed at any time in the Google account: Security → Third-party apps.
